Aurora Photonics Inc. Annual Report on Form 10-K for fiscal 2025 (excerpt)

Item 1. Business. Aurora Photonics designs and manufactures 800G and 1.6T optical transceivers for data-center networks.

Suppliers. Borealis Substrates supplies the bare indium phosphide substrates for our laser chips under a supply agreement that runs through 2027. We qualify each substrate lot in our Fremont fab before use.

We manufacture our transceivers in our own facilities and in those of contract manufacturers.
