Ember Lasers opens second EML laser fab in Tucson

TUCSON, Ariz., May 6, 2025 -- Ember Lasers Inc. today opened its second fab for electro-absorption modulated lasers in Tucson, Arizona, adding capacity for 200G-per-lane EML chips used in 1.6T transceivers.

The 60,000-square-foot facility doubles Ember Lasers' wafer starts and is expected to reach full output in the second half of 2026. Equipment installation finished in April, and the first production wafers were started this week.

"Demand for 200G EML lasers keeps rising with AI cluster build-outs," said Maya Ortiz, chief executive officer of Ember Lasers. "Our second fab gives customers a second site for the chips they depend on."

Ember Lasers designs and manufactures indium phosphide laser chips for data-center and telecom optics. The company is headquartered in Tucson, Arizona.
