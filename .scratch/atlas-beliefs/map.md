## Not yet specified

- **The find step** (the goal review of 2026-10-18, from the debate with an Opus reviewer). The scorecard measures *argue* given the right company. Every positive case was seeded by the owner or by Serenity, so what goes unmeasured is whether Atlas would have looked at SanDisk or IREN at all. Amendment 2 measures it prospectively: memory-packaging's signal precision@20, and each company outside the universe dated when a Candidate or Scout Issue first names it. Nothing builds toward it yet: a ticket after the scorecard's runs, and before a third theme.
- **Confirmation latency.** The case studies' money was in the confirmation (AXT on 10-30, SanDisk on 09-10), not the first hint. `belief_confirmed` notices exist (ticket 26), but nothing measures how long it takes from a confirming document's `available_at` to the notice. That is a small measure on the prospective record.
- **Held-out cases the model does not know.** The closed-book arm (`scorecard/closed-book.md`) found the frozen model knows 2025's famous winners and even names later events. So the session arm cannot discriminate on such cases, and the prospective record (19) is the only clean test of the session. Future held-out cases should be smaller or less-covered names, chosen by rule (not by outcome), and probed closed-book before they enter a key.
- **Scheduling sessions** (in the cluster's agent-lab or as cloud routines) once the judge loop and the pages exist; which questions a scheduled session takes (the Issue frontier) and how its output is reviewed before a judgement.
- **Hypotheses and Beliefs.** A Hypothesis version's findings become Beliefs; whether the Hypothesis stays as the published, snapshotted bundle of Beliefs (likely) or merges into them; the publish gate's Belief rule in detail.
- **Retiring the default plan and the pipeline's edge review** (57% right) once Beliefs are the record; what of Relationships survives as evidence for Beliefs.
- **Switching bulk retention off**: when (after 06), and what the research bank keeps of the 70k extracted facts (nothing is deleted; whether old observations are retired).
- **The second theme** (memory and packaging, ranked first on the pilot map) as the first use of Issues across themes; the layer taxonomy per theme.
- **Trackinizer** as the graph view, when the owner's projection (01) has been judged.
- **The bar for sessions**: the pilot's criteria apply; whether faithfulness and on-question share are reported beside precision (the withdrawn revision proposal) is decided when 09 reports.
